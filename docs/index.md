# Welcome!

I am an electrical engineer based in the Chicagoland area of Illinois. Welcome to my personal website. 

Here you can find my resume and some information about my personal projects. My projects consist of a mix of radio frequency (RF) systems and power systems simulations and computational tools, as I had my start in RF but more recently I am becoming more involved in the power world. I am currently an RF Systems Engineer at Northrop Grumman working toward transitioning full-time into the power industry.

I am learning how to conduct various steady-state power systems analyses, such as power flow, optimal power flow, contingency analysis, voltage stability analysis, and short-circuit analysis, for starters. I am enrolled in a graduate-level course on power system dynamics for Fall 2026 at Iowa State University. These analyses are very useful for ISO/RTOs for their real-time operations and their transmission planning functions. 

I believe this is a great time to get involved in supporting the bulk electric system with the coming wave of large-loads and renewables looking to integrate with the grid.

The future is bright! Let's get to work...
